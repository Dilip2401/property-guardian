Simple by design

One clear workflow. No guesswork.

Property Guardian separates observing a problem from deciding what to do about it.

01 — GUARDIAN

Inspect

A trained Guardian visits on schedule and checks the property room by room.

02 — EVIDENCE

Capture

Findings are supported with photos, timestamps, visit records and clear observations.

03 — ADMIN

Validate

Admin reviews the report and separates routine observations from issues needing your attention.

04 — OWNER

Decide

You approve, decline or ask for more information. Repairs happen only with the right authorization.

What we check
