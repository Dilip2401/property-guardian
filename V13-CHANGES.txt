PROPERTY GUARDIAN V13 — CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

V13 builds on V12 and focuses on the owner/customer experience.

NEW
- Owner Action Center on the customer Overview dashboard.
- Clear contextual states: decisions waiting, information request in progress, inspection under Admin review, or no action required.
- Quick actions to Latest Report, Property Passport and Property History.
- Recent Property Activity panel.
- Health Trend visualization using recorded inspection visits.
- Responsive quick-action layout for smaller screens.
- Preserved V12 logout/login fix and V10 Admin-only owner information-request notifications.

PRINCIPLE
- Keep the owner experience decision-oriented: We inspect. You decide.
- Health trend is an operational indicator, not a structural or insurance guarantee.
