PROPERTY GUARDIAN V31 — ADMIN PROPERTY ONBOARDING

Enhancement requested:
- Added a prominent Admin-only “Add new property” action inside CRM & Control > Properties.
- Added a single guided onboarding workflow with three steps:
  1. Customer — create a new customer or select an existing customer; capture contact, correspondence and emergency details.
  2. Property — capture Property Passport master data, service plan, access instructions, baseline and rooms.
  3. Guardian & visit — assign the Guardian and schedule the first inspection with date, time and visit frequency.
- New property onboarding creates the customer, property, default room records and first Guardian visit as one server-side operation.
- Existing customers can be linked to a new property without overwriting their CRM profile.
- New customer email uniqueness is validated.
- Guardian receives a notification for the newly scheduled first visit.
- Admin audit trail and property history record the onboarding event.
- The older Operations “Create Property Passport” form was replaced by a shortcut into the same controlled CRM onboarding workflow to avoid duplicate property-creation paths.
- Production note: local prototype may show a generated/entered temporary customer password; production should use an invitation/password-reset flow and never expose passwords in alerts or logs.
